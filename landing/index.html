3 · Five guards

Five guards between a message and a refund

1 · UnderstandFinds the payment ID by pattern matching, never by AI guesswork.
2 · Rules, not promptsA policy file decides: refund, ask a human, or stop.
3 · Human gateAbove ₹500, a supervisor must approve with a reason.
4 · Prove itCompares data before and after. No change means not done.
5 · RememberEvery step goes into a chained record that exposes edits.

6 · Under the hood

The tech stack, in plain words

Python + asyncioFetches 5 facts at the same time (a task graph).
YAML policyBusiness rules live in a file anyone can read.
SQLiteSimulated payment systems. No real Paytm or bank APIs.
State-diff verifierSnapshot before, snapshot after, compare.
SHA-256 hash chainEach record is locked to the one before it.
Groq LLM (optional)Only labels intent and polishes wording. Never decides.
StreamlitThe product dashboard and supervisor desk.
pytestTests, including tricks the rules were not written for.
